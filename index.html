rclone-book

rclone-book is a personal tool, used only by its owner. It runs rclone on the owner's own computer to upload, list and organize the owner's own files in Google Drive and Google Sheets.

It is not offered to anyone else, has no server, and does not collect data from other people.

個人用のツールです。持ち主のパソコンで rclone を動かし、持ち主自身の Google ドライブとスプレッドシートのファイルを上げ下ろし・整理するためだけに使います。
